Md Dilshadur Rahman, PhD, is a postdoctoral research fellow in Nils Gehlenborg’s HIDIVE Lab at Harvard Medical School. He received his PhD in Computer Science from the University of Utah, where he studied annotations in visualization and developed tools for creating chart annotations. He also holds an MS in Computer Science from the University of South Florida and a BS in Computer Science from Khulna University of Engineering and Technology.

At Harvard, Dilshadur works at the intersection of biomedical data visualization, human-computer interaction, and AI. He studies how people with different backgrounds and levels of expertise interpret complex biomedical visualizations and communicate their findings. He uses this understanding to develop visualization frameworks and tools that support biomedical data analysis and communication.
